Frequently asked questions

How do I manage or cancel my Parostok+ subscription?

Parostok+ is billed by Apple, so cancellation is handled in the App Store, not inside the app. Open Profile → Subscription → Manage subscription, which takes you straight to the App Store subscription screen. You can also get there via iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the subscription stays active until the end of the paid period.

I paid, but Parostok+ is not active

Open the paywall and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the app with the same account you used when purchasing — the subscription is tied to your Parostok account so it works across your devices. If it still does not appear, email us and mention the Apple ID region and the approximate purchase date.

How do I delete my account and my data?

In the app: Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, your garden, journal entries, and chat history. The action cannot be undone. If you cannot access the app, email us from the address linked to your account and we will delete it for you.

I signed in on a new device and my garden is empty

Your garden is bound to your account, not to the device. Make sure you signed in with the same provider (Apple or Google) that you used originally — signing in with a different provider creates a separate account, even if both use the same email address.

I am not receiving reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for Parostok in iOS Settings → Notifications, and that reminders are switched on in Profile → Settings → Reminders. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device, so they also require the app to have been opened at least once after installation.

A plant I grow is missing, or the care advice looks wrong

Write to us. Care schedules are based on the plant species, your location type (windowsill, balcony, terrace, garden) and local weather, so if something looks off for your conditions, tell us what you grow and where — this is how the plant database gets better.
