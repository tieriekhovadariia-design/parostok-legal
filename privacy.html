2 Data we collect

We keep data collection to what the app actually needs. We collect:

  • Account data — your name (as provided by Apple or Google during sign-in), your email address, and which sign-in method you used (Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google).
  • Content you create in the app — your gardens and selected plants, journal entries, checklists and care logs, onboarding-consultation answers, and the messages you send to the in-app AI assistant ("Пан Тепличний").
  • Your selected growing region — the region you choose yourself from a list inside the app. We do not access your device's GPS or precise location.
  • Subscription status — whether you have an active Parostok Plus subscription or free trial. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never receive or store your card or bank details.
  • Basic technical and security data — such as app and runtime version and limited technical logs generated when you use the app, used to keep the service running securely and to deliver over-the-air updates and (in the future) push notifications.
We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, health data, or advertising identifiers, and we do not run any third-party crash-reporting or analytics SDK. Parostok contains no advertising and no cross-app tracking. If we add any such feature in future, we will update this policy first.

5 Storage on your device

Parostok is a local-first app: much of your data is stored directly on your device so the app works offline. We also use secure device storage to keep you signed in (authentication tokens) and to remember your preferences and progress. This on-device data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-app tracking technologies.

6 International transfers

Your account and content are stored in the European Union (Ireland). Some providers listed above are based in the United States, so sending data to them involves an international transfer. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards permitted by the GDPR, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, together with the security measures those providers apply.

7 How long we keep your data

We keep your account data and content for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we remove your personal data from our live systems promptly. Residual copies contained in encrypted backups are overwritten in the normal backup cycle, within 30 days.

Some data is also stored locally on your device (Parostok works largely offline). Deleting the app removes that local data from your device.

8 Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • erase your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive the personal data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format such as JSON (data portability);
  • withdraw consent where our processing is based on consent, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, email tieriekhova.dariia@gmail.com. We will respond within one month, as required by the GDPR; for complex requests we may extend this by up to two further months and will tell you if we do.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Ukraine this is the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights (Ombudsman); in the EU it is the data protection authority in your country of residence.

9 Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its personal data at any time from within the app: open Profile and choose Delete account. This action is permanent and removes your account and associated data as described in section 7.

Please note: deleting your account does not automatically cancel any active App Store or Google Play subscription. To stop future billing, cancel the subscription in your Apple or Google account settings. If you need help, contact us at the email above.

10 Children

Parostok is not directed to children. You must be at least 16 years old to create an account and use the app, unless a parent or legal guardian provides consent as required in your country. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data without such consent, contact us and we will delete it.

11 Security and data breaches

We protect your data with measures including encryption in transit, access controls that scope your data to your account (row-level security), authenticated access to backend functions, and storage within the European Union. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and keep our safeguards current.

If we become aware of a personal data breach that affects your information, we will notify affected users and the competent authorities where required by applicable law, and take reasonable steps to limit its impact.

12 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. When we make material changes, we will update the date at the top and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use of Parostok after an update means you accept the revised policy.
