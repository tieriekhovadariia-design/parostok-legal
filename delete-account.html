Delete Your Account

Last updated: 14 July 2026

This page explains how to delete your Parostok account and what happens to your data when you do. It applies to the Parostok (Паросток) mobile app, operated by Dariia Tieriekhova. Please read it alongside our Privacy Policy.

1 Delete from within the app

The fastest way to delete your account is directly in the app:

  1. Open Parostok and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the Account section.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and its associated data are deleted immediately and permanently — both on the server and on your device. This action cannot be undone.

2 If you no longer have the app installed

If you have already removed the app and cannot use the in-app option, you can request deletion by email:

Email tieriekhova.dariia@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, with the subject “Delete my Parostok account”.

To help us locate and verify your account, please include the email address and sign-in method (Apple or Google) you used. We will delete your account and confirm by reply, normally within 30 days of receiving the request.

3 What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes the personal data tied to it, including:

  • Your profile details (name and email provided by Apple or Google at sign-in);
  • Your garden, plants, care calendar, and task history;
  • Your journal entries and any photos attached to them;
  • Your AI assistant conversations;
  • Your subscription record on our servers.

Data stored only on your device is also cleared when you delete your account from within the app.

4 What may be retained

After deletion we do not keep your personal account data. We may retain limited information where the law allows or requires it, for example:

  • Anonymized or aggregated usage logs that are not linked to you and cannot identify you;
  • Records we are required to keep for legal, tax, or accounting purposes (such as proof of a purchase), kept for the minimum period required and then deleted.

For full detail on what we collect and why, see our Privacy Policy.

5 Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not by us. To stop future billing, cancel the subscription in your store account settings before or after deleting your account:

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Parostok → Cancel.
  • Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Parostok → Cancel.

6 Contact

Questions about deleting your account or your data? Email tieriekhova.dariia@gmail.com.
